Privacy Policy

Ervin is a personal assistant used by a single person, its owner. No one else uses it.

Ervin accesses Google Calendar data only to show, create, and update the owner's own events at the owner's request.

Calendar data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. It is not transferred to anyone other than the owner.

Ervin's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
